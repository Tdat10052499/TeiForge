# Code of Conduct

TeiForge follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, give constructive feedback,
and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, insults, and personal or
political attacks are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other
project space.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, Teichi
([@Tdat10052499](https://github.com/Tdat10052499)), privately. Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, close threads, or block participants who
break this code of conduct.
